How it works

You talk

In any language. It replies like a good coach: short, direct, one question at a time.

It learns

After each reply it extracts the durable facts — goals, schedule, constraints, progress — and stores each one encrypted in your own space on Walrus.

It recalls

Next time, your message is matched against your memories and the relevant ones shape the reply. Every answer shows what it used.

Open-weight model (Qwen 3.8 27B via Groq) · memory on Walrus Memory, encrypted, no database of you · web search with cited sources when you ask for facts · an AI, not a professional — it can be wrong. Privacy & terms
